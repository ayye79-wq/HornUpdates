About the publication

Independent context for the Horn of Africa

Horn Updates is an independent digital publication focused on Ethiopia, Somalia, Sudan, South Sudan, Eritrea, Djibouti, Kenya and the Red Sea region.

What we publish

We publish explainers, selected analysis, a periodic regional briefing and clearly labelled personal opinion. Our purpose is to make complicated regional developments understandable without presenting speculation as established fact.

Who is responsible

Horn Updates is independently operated and edited by Kalid Kayo. Personal opinion may also appear under contributor names or pen names when privacy or security is a concern. A pen name does not imply credentials, institutional affiliation or field access.

How we work

Editors select topics, review factual claims, add sources and decide what is published. Research and drafting tools may assist parts of the workflow, but automation is not permitted to publish opinion or analysis without editorial review. Legacy automated publishing output was retired in September 2026.

Our limits

Horn Updates is not a wire service and does not claim reporters in every country it covers. We rely on public records, primary documents and credited reporting, then provide context and analysis. Conflict reporting can change quickly; readers should consult linked sources and send corrections when evidence changes.

Funding and independence

The publication may use advertising to support operating costs. Advertisers do not select topics, review articles or influence conclusions. Sponsored material, if introduced, will be labelled prominently.

Contact

Questions, corrections and contributor enquiries can be sent through the contact page or to admin@hornupdates.com.
