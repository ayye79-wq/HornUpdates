About the publication

Context on the Horn of Africa, not just headlines

Horn Updates is an independent publication explaining what is changing in Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa, and why it matters.

What we cover

Our focus is Ethiopia first, then the wider region: Eritrea, Somalia and Somaliland, Sudan, South Sudan, Djibouti, and Kenya where events cross borders. We cover politics, conflict and security, the economy, humanitarian conditions, diplomacy and the Red Sea, and rights and society.

What we publish

The Signal is our weekly briefing: what happened, why it matters and what to watch, with every item linked to its source. We read reporting in Amharic, Afaan Oromoo and Tigrinya as well as English, so readers get context that rarely reaches English-language coverage.

Explainers give lasting background on the region’s big questions, from the GERD to Tigray to Ethiopia’s search for sea access. Opinion and analysis carries signed arguments, clearly labelled and kept separate from reporting.

How we work

Automation helps us discover stories and prepare first drafts. It does not decide what is published. An editor selects every story, checks drafts against their sources and edits them before publication. Contested claims are attributed to whoever makes them, and state media is labelled as such.

We would rather publish a short, well-sourced briefing than a long feed of weakly sourced claims.

A note on AI coverage

From September to October 2026, Horn Updates briefly covered artificial-intelligence news. We have returned to the region the publication was founded to cover.

Who is responsible

Horn Updates is independently operated and edited by Kalid Kayo. Pieces published by the Horn Updates Editorial Desk represent the publication’s edited synthesis of cited sources.

Funding and independence

The publication may use advertising to support operating costs. Advertisers, governments, political organizations and parties to the region’s conflicts do not select topics, review stories or influence conclusions. Sponsored material, if introduced, will be clearly labelled.

Corrections and contact

If a factual claim is wrong or a source has changed, send the page URL and supporting evidence through the contact page or email admin@hornupdates.com.
