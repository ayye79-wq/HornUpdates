About the publication

Useful AI news, not an AI content farm

Horn Updates tracks the artificial-intelligence developments that change what people, companies and institutions can actually do.

What we cover

Our core beats are frontier models, AI agents, products and tools, cybersecurity, business and jobs, policy, infrastructure, and AI developments across Africa. We focus on developments with practical consequences rather than publishing every announcement.

How we work

Automation helps discover candidate stories and organize sources. It does not automatically decide what becomes news. Public articles are selected from identifiable sources, checked against primary material whenever possible, and edited to separate reported facts from Horn Updates analysis.

We prefer a shorter verified feed over a larger feed padded with weakly sourced claims. When a primary source is a company announcing its own product, we say so and treat its performance claims as vendor claims unless independent evidence is available.

Why the name Horn Updates?

The publication began with a focus on the Horn of Africa. The editorial focus changed to artificial intelligence in September 2026, but the name remains—and the geographic perspective remains useful. Africa is often treated as an afterthought in global technology coverage. Horn Updates keeps a dedicated Africa & AI beat covering infrastructure, startups, local-language systems, education, jobs, regulation and digital sovereignty.

Who is responsible

Horn Updates is independently operated and edited by Kalid Kayo. Articles published by the Horn Updates Editorial Desk represent the publication's edited synthesis of cited sources, not the views of a source company.

Funding and independence

The publication may use advertising to support operating costs. Advertisers do not select topics, review stories or influence conclusions. Sponsored material, if introduced, will be clearly labeled.

Corrections and contact

If a factual claim is wrong or a source has changed, send the article URL and supporting evidence through the contact page or email admin@hornupdates.com.
