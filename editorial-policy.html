Editorial policy

Coverage of a polarized region is useful only if readers can tell what is known, who said it and where our interpretation begins.

Every claim has a source

Each item in The Signal links the reporting or official material it rests on. We prefer primary material (official statements, documents, court filings, UN and humanitarian data) and reputable independent reporting, including outlets publishing in Amharic, Afaan Oromoo and Tigrinya.

Contested claims are attributed

Casualty figures, territorial control, accusations and claims of responsibility are attributed to whoever makes them, whether a government, an armed group, a party or an outlet. When only one side’s account is available, we say so. We use neutral names for groups and places rather than adopting one side’s labels.

State media is labelled

Reporting from state or state-affiliated media, such as the Ethiopian News Agency or Fana, is labelled as state media and treated as the official position, not as independent confirmation.

Protecting people at risk

We do not name private individuals who could face harm from being identified, including victims, witnesses, minors and detained people who are not public figures.

Automation and AI assistance

Automation monitors approved sources and places candidate links in an editorial inbox. AI tools may help organize research, translate and summarize sources, and prepare first drafts. Discovery is not publication: an editor selects every story, checks each draft against its source and edits it before it is published.

No synthetic volume

Horn Updates does not aim to maximize article count. We do not publish mass-produced paraphrases of other outlets, fabricated quotes, invented sources or pages whose main purpose is search traffic. When there is no worthwhile update, publishing nothing is an acceptable editorial decision.

Fact, analysis and opinion

Reported facts are traceable to cited material. Analysis, such as “why it matters” and “what to watch”, explains implications and is identified by its framing. Personal opinion is labelled and reflects the named contributor rather than the publication.

Corrections

Request a correction at admin@hornupdates.com with the page URL and supporting evidence. Material changes are corrected on the page and the updated date is changed.

Independence and advertising

Advertisers, governments, political organizations and parties to the region’s conflicts receive no editorial approval over Horn Updates coverage. Advertising is kept separate from editorial content, and sponsored content, if introduced, will be clearly labelled.
