Editorial policy

These standards apply to every article that remains in the public Horn Updates index.

Labels and attribution

Reported facts, institutional analysis and personal opinion are presented as different forms. Opinion represents the named contributor’s judgment. Organizational analysis is attributed to the Horn Updates Editorial Desk. Pen names may be used for privacy or security, but are not presented as evidence of professional credentials.

Sources and uncertainty

Material factual claims should be supported by primary documents or credited reporting wherever practical. We identify disputed claims, distinguish inference from fact and link readers to supporting material. External links are selected for relevance, not endorsement.

Automation and AI assistance

Research, transcription, translation, coding and drafting tools may assist editors. They do not make final publication decisions. Opinion and analysis must be selected, reviewed and published by a responsible editor. In September 2026, Horn Updates disabled its automated opinion generator and retired the repetitive daily output it had produced.

Corrections

Readers may request a correction by emailing admin@hornupdates.com with the page URL and supporting evidence. Material corrections are added to the relevant article; dates are updated when an article changes substantially.

Independence and advertising

Editorial decisions are not made by governments, political organizations, advertisers or technology providers. Advertising is separated from editorial content. We do not ask readers to click advertisements or use artificial traffic and engagement services.

Publication limits

We do not claim eyewitness reporting or field access unless an article states and substantiates it. Fast-moving political and conflict information may become outdated; publication dates and source links help readers evaluate that limitation.
