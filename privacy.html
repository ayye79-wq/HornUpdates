Privacy policy

Horn Updates does not require an account to read public content. This policy explains the information that may be processed when you visit the site or voluntarily contact or subscribe to us.

Information you submit

If you use a contact, correction, advertising or newsletter form, we may receive information you provide such as your name, email address, company, message and subscription request. We use that information only for the purpose for which it was submitted and related administration.

Analytics and technical data

Hosting, security and analytics services may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring URLs, approximate location and visit times. Horn Updates uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage and improve the publication.

Cookies and consent

Optional analytics and advertising storage are controlled by the site's consent mechanism. Until consent is granted where required, Horn Updates signals denied storage to Google services. You may also manage cookies through your browser.

Advertising

Horn Updates may use Google AdSense or other advertising providers. Advertising providers may use cookies, device information and related technologies subject to their own policies and your consent choices. Google's advertising controls are available through Google Ads Settings.

Newsletter and contact providers

Contact and newsletter submissions may be processed by service providers used to receive forms, host the site or deliver email. We do not sell or rent subscriber or contact information.

Sponsorship inquiries

If you contact Horn Updates about sponsorship or advertising, the business information you submit may be retained as reasonably necessary to discuss, administer or document that commercial relationship.

External links

Horn Updates links to external sources, research papers, companies and publishers. Those sites operate under their own privacy and security practices.

Retention and security

We keep submitted information only as long as reasonably needed for the requested service, business records, legal obligations, security or abuse prevention. No internet system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information we control. Email admin@hornupdates.com and we will respond or route the request to the relevant service provider when appropriate.

Children

Horn Updates is a general-audience news publication and does not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the publication or its service providers change. Material revisions will be reflected in the updated date above.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to admin@hornupdates.com or through the contact page.
